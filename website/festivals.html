Famous Indian Festivals

Diwali

Diwali is known as the Festival of Lights. People decorate their homes with lamps and celebrate with family.

Holi

Holi is known as the Festival of Colors. People play with colors and enjoy sweets with friends and family.

Dasara

Dasara is a famous festival celebrated in India. It represents the victory of good over evil.

Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi is celebrated to worship Lord Ganesha with joy and devotion.

Pongal

Pongal is a harvest festival mainly celebrated in Tamil Nadu. People prepare traditional Pongal dishes and decorate homes.

Onam

Onam is a famous harvest festival of Kerala. It is celebrated with flower decorations, traditional food and cultural activities.

Christmas

Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ. People decorate Christmas trees, exchange gifts and enjoy time with family.

Janmashtami

Janmashtami celebrates the birth of Lord Krishna. Devotees celebrate with prayers, songs and cultural programs. It is celebrated with great joy across india.

Raksha Bandhan

Raksha Bandhan celebrates the special bond between brothers and sisters by tying a Rakhi.

Eid

Eid is celebrated by Muslims with prayers, family gatherings, traditional food and sharing happiness with others.

Navratri

Navratri is a nine-night festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. Garba and Dandiya are popular celebrations.

Baisakhi

Baisakhi is an important harvest festival of Punjab. It is celebrated with traditional dances, music and food.

Ramadan

Ramadan is a holy month observed by Muslims around the world. It is a time of fasting, prayer, charity and self-disciplane.

Good Friday

Good Friday is an important christian religious day that remebers the death of death of Jesus Christ. It is observed on the Friday before Easter Sunday.

Ugadi

Ugadi is a traditional New Year festival celebrated in Karnataka. People decorate their homes and celebrate with joy and prayers.

Buddha Purnima

Buddha Purnima is an important Buddhist festival celebrated in India. It marks the birth of Gautama Buddha and teaches peace, kindness.
