National Festivals

National festivals are important celebrations that bring people across India together and remind us of the country's history, values and achievements. These festivals are celebrated with respect and enthusiasm throughout the country. Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti are important national occasions that help people remember India's Constitution, independence and the contribution of Mahatma Gandhi.
